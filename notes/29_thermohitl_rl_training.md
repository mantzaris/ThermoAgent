# Learned allocation and RL training disposition

The planned primary learned methods were `learned_no_thermodynamics` and
`thermohitl_rl`, with at least five independent initialization/training seeds,
fixed budgets, and prospective checkpoint selection. The trigger would control
communication/oversight eligibility while a compact policy selected among
continue, targeted coordination, information, recommendation, approval,
conflict-resolution, and emergency-override requests.

No v3 learned method was trained. Gate 5 failed before training, so there are
zero training seeds and zero checkpoints. `training/NOT_RUN.json`,
`checkpoints/NOT_TRAINED.json`, `tables/rl_training_seeds.csv`, and the explicit
`training_seed_curves.pdf` NOT-RUN panel prevent absent data from being mistaken
for stable learning curves.

V1/v2 checkpoints remain frozen antecedents and were not relabeled as v3 seeds.
