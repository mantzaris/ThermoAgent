# Development findings and counterfactual mechanism

Fixed communication versus no communication reduced paired aggregate loss by
48.45% commercial (bootstrap 95% CI 42.31–54.86%) and 52.54% humanitarian
(44.90–60.72%). The deliberately actionable v3 environment therefore corrects
v2's absent coordination-necessity problem at development level.

KPI-triggered bounded simulated supervision versus autonomy reduced loss by
1.59% commercial (1.02–2.17%) and 2.80% humanitarian (1.40–4.37%). These are
development effects, not human evidence.

For `thermohitl_rule`, paired intervention probes show 98 complete causal chains
commercially and 80 humanitarianly. The operator action changed an agent action
or commitment, an accepted material action followed, material reached demand,
and the primary outcome changed. Five humanitarian probes were harmful; 30
commercial and 147 humanitarian probes had no primary benefit. KPI-triggered
probes produced 36 and 32 complete chains. These null/harmful cases remain in
the ledger and figures.

The final trigger-development set averaged 7.33 requests, 3.97 completed
interventions, and 38.2 simulated-operator minutes per disrupted episode. It was
neither inactive nor always-on. Still, timing/actionability do not establish
incremental thermodynamic information value.
