## Prohibited language

Do not claim:

- that v4 is validated or confirmatory;
- that an AIJ contribution is empirically established;
- that “human operators” improved outcomes without the word *simulated*;
- real-human usability, trust, workload, safety, or fatigue evidence;
- real utility operational/cybersecurity validity;
- literal physical energy, entropy, temperature, or free energy;
- universal thermodynamic value;
- validation, five-seed RL, or holdout results;
- communication necessity in all applications; or
- that 4.432% passes a 5% prospective threshold.

The strongest accurate synthesis is: development evidence suggests that
distributed belief observability may add causal triage value under fragmented
information in humanitarian and abstract utility-restoration settings, but a
prerequisite coordination gate failed and no confirmatory stage was run.
