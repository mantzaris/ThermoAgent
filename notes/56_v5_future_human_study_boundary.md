# V5 future human-study boundary

No person was recruited, observed, or evaluated. Every operator result in V5
comes from a simulated bounded operator or an oracle analysis control. The
dashboard demonstrates technical replay and information-boundary enforcement;
it does not establish usability, workload, trust, comprehension, or real-world
decision quality.

A future study should compare KPI-only against the identical KPI payload plus
entropy, disagreement, and consensus confidence under a fixed attention
budget. Humanitarian and abstract utility-restoration tasks are primary;
commercial remains a boundary task. It should preregister one primary endpoint
(for example, causal service loss avoided per active operator minute), block
and counterbalance matched panels, cluster by participant and environment,
measure harmful choices and response time, and include a validated workload
instrument such as NASA-TLX.

Institutional ethics/IRB review or determination, informed consent, data
governance, accessibility review, and a separate security/privacy review are
required before recruitment. V5 simulated effect sizes must not be used as if
they were human pilot estimates.
