# V6 architecture, entropy, and information boundaries

V6 changes the control target from direct action ranking to selective
delegation. Level 1 remains an independent organization-owned operational
proposal. Level 2 decides whether that proposal may execute, should trigger
peer evidence, should be deferred/abstained, or should enter the bounded
simulated-operator queue.

Every incident has three autonomous organizations and every panel has four
concurrent incidents. Agents retain private observations, beliefs, memories,
utilities, inboxes, commitments, and role masks. Delivered sketches contain
only probability summaries, local confidence, age, sender, recipient, and
modeled reliability. True incident mode, correct action, random tape,
counterfactual effects, and final outcomes are evaluator-only. Partition logic
prevents delivery rather than merely changing a feature.

The primary information distinction is aleatoric local uncertainty versus
epistemic cross-agent disagreement. Shannon, five fixed Tsallis orders,
Gini–Simpson, Jensen–Shannon, Jensen–Tsallis, graph-weighted disagreement,
consensus residual, and temporal changes are recorded. q=2 and Gini–Simpson
are mathematically redundant and never interpreted as independent predictors
inside the same primary block. Energy and free energy remain secondary,
dimensionless diagnostics.

The operator view is the exact hashed deployable context. Dashboard replay is
GPU-free and shows provenance, contributors, stale/missing agents, the action
proposal and alternatives, operator queue, minutes, and uncertainty
decomposition. Counterfactual outcomes appear only in an analysis view, never
the normal operator view.
