# V6 future human-study boundary

No real human-subject study is authorized or performed. All current operator
actions, workload, latency, and minutes are simulated. The prospective study
template is stored at
`results/generalized_entropic_consensus_v6/protocol/FUTURE_HUMAN_STUDY.md`.
Any future deployment requires ethics/IRB review, participant consent, privacy
review, accessibility testing, application-specific safety review, and a new
protocol that treats participants—not operator clicks—as independent units.
