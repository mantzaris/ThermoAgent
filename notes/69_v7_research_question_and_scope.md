## Research question

When does distributed generalized-entropic consensus become useful for
communication-efficient monitoring and risk-controlled coordination among
independent autonomous agents in complex, coupled, partially observed
networks?

The co-primary estimands are (1) the controller-by-coupling-by-fragmentation
interaction in harmful-action rate and (2) matched-coverage harm and service
effects in a prospectively fixed high-complexity region. Communication
efficiency is co-primary only for fully counted traffic.

Humanitarian multi-commodity logistics and abstract defensive utility
restoration are separate domain simulators. Commercial logistics is historical
or optional boundary evidence and will not dominate compute.

V7 is not authorized to claim literal thermodynamics, operational utility
validation, real-human effectiveness, or offensive cyber capability.
