# V7 entropy and macrostate definitions

V7 reuses the numerically tested normalized Shannon, Tsallis q in
{0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 3}, Gini-Simpson, reliability-weighted pooling,
Jensen-Shannon/Jensen-Tsallis disagreement, graph disagreement, consensus, and
temporal slopes from V6. These are dimensionless information-theoretic
quantities, not literal thermodynamic state variables.

New secondary macrostates include graph spectral entropy, allocation entropy,
and economic Gini inequality. Gini-Simpson impurity and economic Gini are kept
under different names and interpretations.

Distributed weights use only telemetry confidence, message age, edge
reliability, trust, latency, and availability. They do not use true incident
mode, correct action, future loss, or counterfactual utility. Every sketch
transmission is an actual message with bytes, latency, delivery or drop, and an
edge-level account.

Application outcomes never read entropy. Entropy is eligible only for Level-2
risk, communication, abstention, and escalation decisions. Energy and free
energy remain secondary and are not part of the current V7 primary mechanism.
