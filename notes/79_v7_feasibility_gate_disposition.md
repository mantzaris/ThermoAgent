# V7 feasibility gate disposition

This note was written after retained feasibility-pilot outcomes and before
the formal protocol freeze or any formal-development episode. It does not
promote pilot findings to formal evidence.

## Gate result

Prospectively recorded feasibility Gates A, B, and C passed. The evidence is
in `results/complexity_entropic_coordination_v7/development/gate_feasibility/`.
The gates establish engineering integrity, an actionable mixed beneficial and
harmful action pool, multiple physical actions, multi-stage causal chains,
cross-community traffic, nonconstant information measures, and absence of a
near-perfect single-feature label proxy. They deliberately did not require a
positive entropic-controller effect.

Across the 45 retained smoke and pilot ledgers, replay found zero mismatches,
zero privacy failures, and maximum independently reconstructed conservation
residual `1.3002932064409833e-12`. The system-Python repository suite passed
378 tests. A separately retained isolated-project-venv run failed 12 tests
solely because that local venv lacks PyTorch; this environment issue is not
silently reclassified as a software pass.

## Pilot findings and interpretation

The final ten-episode pilot yielded 537 accepted evaluator probes. The
humanitarian action pool contained 69 beneficial and 99 harmful probes; the
utility pool contained 41 beneficial and 328 harmful probes. Five physical
action types appeared in each application. KPI-only harm AUC was 0.503
humanitarian and 0.650 utility, while the simple Shannon-plus-disagreement
score AUC was 0.611 and 0.471, respectively.

The pilot effect was not favorable evidence. Humanitarian high-complexity
incremental harm reduction was approximately `+0.0233`, accompanied by net
utility change `-0.0346`; the pooled coupling-by-fragmentation coefficient was
`-0.05879`. These values were inspected only to diagnose feasibility and are
not used as formal estimates or to weaken the protocol.
