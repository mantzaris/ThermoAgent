# V8 replacement formal-development rule

Recorded before execution of replacement seeds 8813001 onward. This batch
replaces, rather than pools with, the retained hysteresis-suppressed attempt.
The same 48-panel, two-application design and the same declared threshold and
KPI candidates are used; a +3000 environment-seed offset provides untouched
stochastic tapes and graph instances.

The earlier resource/error eligibility criteria remain unchanged. In addition,
each application must show at least one generalized-information-score
transmission and those transmissions must be at least 5% of all non-initial,
non-partition-recovery transmissions. This is a mechanism-integrity criterion,
not an outcome threshold. A scheduler dominated by maximum-silence refreshes
cannot be selected as the generalized-information method.

No validation or holdout configuration exists at this point. Formal protocol
freeze, autonomous-policy training, and downstream evaluation remain locked
until the replacement batch passes the written rule.
