\author{Anonymous working manuscript}

\begin{abstract}
We study a finite interacting system whose microscopic variables are realized
choices made by state-separated, locally informed large-language-model agent
instances. Agent $i$ owns a
categorical belief $b_i\in\{-1,+1\}$, a committed action
$a_i\in\{-1,+1\}$, confidence, commitment, bounded private memory, a local
field, workload, inbox, outbox, and typed authority. A pinned instruction
model generates random-sequential updates using only local state and delivered
messages. We connect these microdynamics to collective order, fluctuations,
entropy rate, multi-information, an effective symmetric-layer energy,
relaxation, and path-reversal divergence. Earlier discovery and prospective
replication studies found memory effects of \VTwelveMemory{} and
\VThirteenMemory{} nats per attempted update, respectively. We then execute a
prospectively frozen field-quench and restoration study comprising
\VFourteenDecisions{} decisions in \VFourteenTrajectories{} trajectories over
\VFourteenClusters{} new matched graph clusters. Field reversal changes maximum
post-quench macrostate departure relative to nominal evolution by
\VFourteenHtwo{} (95\% interval \VFourteenHtwoCI). The historical frozen
maximum-minus-final restoration coordinate is \VFourteenHthree{}
(\VFourteenHthreeCI), but its structurally nonnegative sign cannot provide a
valid directional test. The full
statistical-mechanical representation changes cluster-held-out balanced
accuracy relative to order-only variables by \VFourteenHfour{}
(\VFourteenHfourCI), although the simple-uncertainty representation attains the
same mean balanced accuracy as the full representation. All six field-reversal
clusters recover below their held-out-cluster-excluded training-nominal thresholds six sweeps after
restoration; the frozen peak-minus-late H3 sign statistic is structurally
nonnegative and is interpreted only with the full recovery path. These
finite-size, single-model results show what is and is not retained by a reduced
statistical-mechanical description. Reference energy is an effective
observable, decoding noise is not physical temperature, and path-reversal
divergence is coarse-grained temporal asymmetry rather than exact thermodynamic
entropy production.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} statistical mechanics of agents; kinetic Ising
models; large language models; entropy rate; irreversibility; quench dynamics.

\section{Introduction}

Networks of autonomous language-model agents are emerging interacting
many-body systems. Each component uses a complicated language-conditioned
stochastic rule, retains private state, receives only selected messages, and
acts with bounded local authority. The microscopic rule is not a spin-flip
formula, yet the network produces familiar collective phenomena: alignment,
fragmentation, fluctuations, persistence, and recovery after changes in fields
or links. A central question for statistical physics is therefore not whether a
thermodynamic metaphor can be attached after simulation, but whether a compact
set of well-defined observables links local response to collective trajectories
and makes distinct regimes quantitatively interpretable.

The kinetic Ising model demonstrates how stochastic local updates generate
order and relaxation \cite{glauber1963}. Logit dynamics has a related
equilibrium interpretation when strategic interactions derive from a potential
\cite{blume1993}. Away from detailed balance, state-space currents and path
reversal distinguish stationary irreversibility \cite{schnakenberg1976,
seifert2005,seifert2012}. These exact constructions provide useful references,
but an LLM network violates several assumptions: the visible binary state may
not close under the dynamics; natural-language messages can carry hidden
semantic variables; memory induces history dependence; and decoding
temperature controls token sampling rather than a physical bath.

Recent studies have nevertheless found compact collective structure in LLM
populations. Network topology and social weighting alter conformity
\cite{han2026}; intrinsic field bias can dominate effective cooperation
\cite{denobili2026}; noise and conformity produce finite-size debate
crossovers \cite{okawa2026}; population size changes basins of attraction
\cite{flint2025}; and large collections of communities exhibit a small number
of dynamical regimes predicted by a fitted statistical model \cite{el2026}.
The present study is complementary. It preserves explicit private-state and
message boundaries, separates belief from executed action, measures
trajectory-level information and fluctuation observables, separates memory
discovery from prospective replication, and applies prospectively frozen
quenches and counter-quenches to new interacting-agent clusters.

Our objective is characterization, not performance optimization. We ask how
bounded memory changes temporal asymmetry; how a field quench and restoration
trace paths through a common reduced state; whether topology and channel
perturbations follow the same path; and which information is missed by order
or uncertainty summaries alone. No observable is required to improve task
reward, and no human or field-system inference is made.

\section{Statistical mechanics of agent collectives}

Statistical mechanics is useful here at three distinct levels. First, realized
binary choices define a discrete configuration on a graph. Second, empirical
response functions can be approximated by kinetic models and analyzed by
mean-field or stability methods. Third, order, fluctuations, entropy, energy,
correlations, and time asymmetry reduce a high-dimensional trajectory to a
small set of interpretable coordinates. Only the first two ingredients are
needed for a rigorous finite stochastic process; no assertion that an LLM is a
physical thermodynamic object follows.

Information quantities retain their literal mathematical meanings. Shannon
entropy measures uncertainty of a specified distribution \cite{shannon1948}.
Total correlation measures dependence not captured by marginal entropies
\cite{watanabe1960}. Transfer entropy measures directed predictive information
\cite{schreiber2000}; it is not entropy production. For a stationary Markov
chain with observed transition kernel, probability-current entropy production
has a precise definition. When visible variables omit history, forward/reverse
path divergence is instead a coarse-grained lower-bound diagnostic
\cite{roldan2012,kawaguchi2013,busiello2019}. Memory and hidden slow variables
can change the thermodynamic bookkeeping of a reduced process
\cite{kutvonen2015}, while finite-state time-asymmetry remains constrained by
cycle affinities in models where those affinities are defined
\cite{liang2023}. Finite-sample entropy estimators are biased,
particularly under sparse support, so all such quantities require fixed
coarse-graining and null floors \cite{paninski2003}.

\subsection{Relation to kinetic, nonreciprocal, and quench models}

The closest analytical reference is an asymmetric kinetic Ising system, for
which mean-field closures can be organized around response functions and
delayed correlations \cite{aguilera2021}. Our measured transition rule differs
in two ways. Its coefficients are induced by a language-conditioned model
rather than specified as a spin kernel, and belief and action form distinct
but coupled layers. We therefore test a fitted kinetic closure against direct
trajectories instead of assuming it by construction.

Nonreciprocal couplings can generate phenomena absent from equilibrium
Hamiltonian systems \cite{fruchart2021}, and their entropy production can be
defined for suitable stochastic dynamics \cite{zhang2023}. The immutable V12
study used this theory as a reference but found no reliable incremental
irreversibility from degree- and traffic-matched directionality in the actual
LLM trajectories. V13 preserved that boundary rather than searching new
directed graphs, and V14 does not reopen it. The nonequilibrium perturbations
are controlled quenches in fields, active delivery support, and packet content. These are
finite classical stochastic trajectories; quantum-quench results
\cite{calabrese2006} supply terminology about relaxation but not a claimed
mapping of microscopic dynamics.

\section{State-separated, locally informed LLM-agent microdynamics}

\subsection{Observable state and autonomy}

The local state is
\begin{equation}
x_i(t)=\bigl(b_i(t),a_i(t),c_i(t),q_i(t),m_i(t),w_i(t)\bigr),
\end{equation}
where $b_i,a_i\in\{-1,+1\}$ are categorical belief and action, $c_i$ is
bounded confidence, $q_i$ a categorical commitment, $m_i$ bounded memory, and
$w_i$ local workload. Each persistent identity separately owns these variables,
a private field $h_i$, an inbox, outbox, role, and typed authority. The global
record $X_t=(x_1,\ldots,x_N)$ is available only to the evaluator.

At each attempted update a random permutation schedule offers one agent a turn.
Its prompt is rebuilt from its private observation, local state, explicitly
delivered packets, and bounded memory when enabled. The model chooses belief,
action, confidence, commitment, memory state, outgoing categorical signal, and
tool. One syntax-repair attempt is permitted; a persistently invalid response
leaves the state unchanged rather than invoking a centralized scientific
choice. One sweep equals $N$ attempted updates.

The model is Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct at the pinned revision reported in the data
package \cite{qwen2024}. The primary regime carries no hidden chat history:
the prompt is fully reconstructed from recorded current state. A secondary
regime adds exactly three local memory summaries. Neither construction proves
Markov closure, which is tested empirically.

\subsection{Latent-label counterbalancing}

Displayed alternatives ``amber'' and ``cobalt'' are semantically symmetric.
Their order and mapping to latent $\pm1$ spins are counterbalanced, private
fields and disordered starts are balanced, and all responses are decoded to the
latent coordinate before analysis. Matched factor and quench arms share graph,
initial state, update schedule, display mapping, and inference seeds. Thus a
coupling or disruption contrast does not change option identity or message
opportunity.

\section{Individual response law}

The microscopic grid varies private field $h_i$, prior belief and action,
delivered neighbor field, coupling $J$, and decoding-noise setting $\tau$. The
primary measured responses are realized categorical choices; reported
confidence is secondary and is not interpreted as a calibrated probability.
We fit
\begin{align}
\Pr[b_i(t+1)=+1\mid X_t]&=\operatorname{logit}^{-1}
(\beta_0+\beta_hh_i+\beta_nJ\bar b_{\partial i}
+\beta_bb_i+\beta_aa_i),\\
\Pr[a_i(t+1)=+1\mid X_t]&=\operatorname{logit}^{-1}
(\gamma_0+\gamma_bb_i(t+1)+\gamma_aa_i(t)).
\end{align}
The model is descriptive. It supplies susceptibilities and a surrogate, not a
normative Bayesian agent or a claim that token temperature equals an effective
thermodynamic temperature.

\section{Effective kinetic and mean-field models}

For a homogeneous approximation, let $m_b$ and $m_a$ be layer means. Replacing
the local neighbor field by $m_b$ yields
\begin{align}
m_b(t+1)&\simeq 2f_b(h+Jm_b(t),m_b(t),m_a(t))-1,\\
m_a(t+1)&\simeq 2f_a(m_b(t+1),m_a(t))-1.
\end{align}
Linearization of this two-dimensional map gives a local stability matrix. The
belief component contains the fitted slope multiplied by $J$, while persistence
and belief--action coupling modify both eigenvalues. We report the local slope
as a crossover diagnostic, not a critical exponent.

Writing the fitted logits as
\begin{align}
u_b&=\beta_0+\beta_hh+\beta_nJm_b+\beta_bm_b+\beta_am_a,\\
u_a&=\gamma_0+\gamma_b\widetilde m_b+\gamma_am_a,
\end{align}
and $s(u)=2(1+e^{-u})^{-1}-1$, the homogeneous map is
$\widetilde m_b=s(u_b)$ and $\widetilde m_a=s(u_a)$. Since
$s'(u)=2p(u)[1-p(u)]$, its local Jacobian is
\begin{equation}
\mathsf J=
\begin{pmatrix}
s'(u_b)(\beta_nJ+\beta_b) & s'(u_b)\beta_a\\
s'(u_a)\gamma_b s'(u_b)(\beta_nJ+\beta_b) &
s'(u_a)[\gamma_b s'(u_b)\beta_a+\gamma_a]
\end{pmatrix}.
\end{equation}
The spectral radius $\rho(\mathsf J)$ is a local persistence diagnostic. A
value near one predicts slow response of the fitted homogeneous surrogate; it
does not prove singular behavior in either the finite surrogate or the LLM
system.

The CPU surrogate applies the fitted stochastic response to independent agents
on ring and fixed-degree modular graphs for $N=8,16,32,64$. It densely maps
coupling and decision-noise settings with 16 graph/seed realizations per cell.
Direct LLM observations remain the empirical anchors: the surrogate can explain
trend directions or miss them, but cannot replace those trajectories.

\section{Macroscopic observables}

Belief and action order are
\begin{equation}
m_b=N^{-1}\sum_i b_i,\qquad m_a=N^{-1}\sum_i a_i,
\end{equation}
and cross-layer overlap is $q_{ba}=N^{-1}\sum_i b_ia_i$. Disagreement is the
fraction of undirected support edges joining unlike spins. Finite-size belief
susceptibility is $\chi_b=N\operatorname{Var}(m_b)$, without a temperature
normalization. Integrated autocorrelation time is calculated from the positive
sequence of the magnetization autocorrelation.

The fixed symmetric-layer reference energy is
\begin{equation}
H_{\rm ref}=-\frac{J_b}{2}\bm b^TW_s\bm b
-\frac{J_a}{2}\bm a^TD_s\bm a-K\bm a^T\bm b
-\bm h^T\bm b-\bm g^T\bm a,
\end{equation}
with the reported coordinate $e_{\rm ref}=H_{\rm ref}/N$. The communication
partition changes active delivery but not this reference layer. We call
$N\operatorname{Var}(e_{\rm ref})$ an energy-fluctuation observable, not
physical heat capacity.

For large networks, a fixed macrostate binning of
$(m_b,m_a,q_{ba},e_{\rm ref},D)$ defines configuration entropy and a
first-history conditional entropy rate. Local-word entropy uses each node's
belief, action, and neighbor majority. Total correlation is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal T=\sum_iH(X_i)-H(X_1,\ldots,X_N),
\end{equation}
estimated over a five-sweep rolling window. These quantities answer different
questions: marginal disorder may be high while dependence is also high, and an
entropy increase may represent adaptation, fragmentation, or transient
uncertainty depending on order and energy.

The evaluator's reduced coordinate is
\begin{equation}
Z_t=(m_b,m_a,q_{ba},S_{\rm config},h_{\rm rate},\mathcal T,
e_{\rm ref},v_E,\chi_b,\rho,D).
\end{equation}
It is a prospectively fixed observable representation, not a complete
thermodynamic state.

The direct trajectories make this distinction concrete. During the
field-reversal interval, mean absolute belief magnetization remains only
$0.0042$, close to the nominal value $0.0097$, because the counterbalanced
private fields cancel at the population mean. Nevertheless, rolling
configuration entropy rises from $0.223$ to $0.387$ nats, entropy rate from
$0.070$ to $0.356$ nats per sweep, raw total correlation from $0.274$ to $4.909$
nats, and the energy-fluctuation observable from $0.010$ to $1.162$. The raw
total-correlation contrast is strongly finite-window dependent: at the primary
five-sweep window its field-minus-nominal value is $4.635$ nats, the
marginal-preserving circular-shift null floor is $4.058$ nats, and the
untruncated adjusted contrast is $0.576$ nats. Its normalized contrast is
$-0.043$, so no absolute dependence scale is inferred. A count or mean-spin
representation nevertheless misses a reorganized but globally balanced
collective configuration.

\section{Prospective design and inference}

V12 remains the immutable discovery study. It found that coupling and decoding
noise changed order, susceptibility, and persistence, that bounded memory
increased adjusted block irreversibility by \VTwelveMemory{} nats per update,
and that traffic- and degree-matched nonreciprocity did not reliably add
irreversibility. V13 independently tested those directions. Its coupling and
noise contrasts did not replicate, whereas its memory contrast was
\VThirteenMemory{} nats per update in the discovery direction. Its four-cluster
quench analysis also showed a large but metric-dependent field-reversal
departure and only preliminary representation separation.

V14 does not rerun the broad factor grid. Before any new formal outcome, we
froze six independent $N=16$ modular graph/environment clusters. Within each
cluster, nominal evolution, field reversal, removal of inter-community
delivery, and corruption of exactly half of eligible categorical packets share
the graph, private fields, initial state, update schedule, display mapping, and
inference seeds. Every trajectory contains 15 baseline, 15 perturbation, and 15
restoration sweeps. The field-reversal contrast is primary; partition and
corruption are secondary comparisons.

The inferential unit is the matched graph/environment cluster. Updates, agents,
messages, rolling windows, and tokens are not independent replicates. H2 tests
field-reversal maximum departure minus nominal. H3 tests early counter-quench
departure minus the final-five-sweep mean. H4 tests the full-minus-order-only
leave-one-cluster-out balanced-accuracy contrast. Each uses exhaustive
one-sided cluster sign flips, 10,000 cluster-bootstrap interval replicates, and
Holm correction across H2--H4. H1 is the already completed V12 discovery--V13
replication memory contrast and is not relabelled as a new V14 experiment.
The H3 peak-minus-late-mean contrast is structurally nonnegative except for a
constant recovery path. We therefore retain its frozen numerical analysis but
do not treat its sign-flip probability alone as evidence of relaxation;
threshold crossing, final residual displacement, and the complete recovery
trajectory carry the scientific interpretation.

\section{Entropy and effective-energy representation}

Order and energy are correlated but not interchangeable. Configurations with
similar magnetization can differ in domain arrangement, action alignment,
multi-information, and temporal predictability. Likewise, a shift in reference
energy can arise from field mismatch, action--belief inconsistency, or spatial
disagreement. Joint energy--entropy trajectories therefore expose degrees of
freedom hidden by state counts alone.

We omit a scalar free-energy coordinate. The microscopic response provides a
decision-noise control and fitted slopes, but no unique, reproducible physical
temperature mapping. The two-dimensional energy--entropy plane is therefore
more transparent and avoids implying a Lyapunov function.

\section{Persistent memory and irreversibility}

For the frozen macrostate word $Y_t$, the length-$L$ reversal statistic is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal I_L=\frac{1}{L-1}\KL[p(Y_t,\ldots,Y_{t+L-1})\Vert
p(Y_{t+L-1},\ldots,Y_t)].
\end{equation}
We subtract the mean from 200 time-shuffled paths of identical length and state
occupancy. The result is reported in nats per attempted update. We also measure
history-order conditional mutual information. Nonzero history dependence means
that $\mathcal I_L$ remains a coarse-grained irreversibility diagnostic, not an
exact total entropy-production rate.

The immutable V12 discovery effect was \VTwelveMemory{} nats/update and the V13
prospective replication effect was \VThirteenMemory{} nats/update. They are
reported separately and combined only in a labelled descriptive
inverse-variance synthesis. Field, graph, schedule, mapping, initial state, and
inference seeds were shared within each memory pair. Fixed block lengths two,
three, and four, shuffled bias floors, entropy rates, autocorrelation, and
conditional mutual information diagnose whether the effect reflects persistent
history rather than state occupancy alone. No new V14 memory trajectory enters
either confidence interval.

Numerically, the discovery estimate was $0.01790$ nats/update (95\% interval
$0.00357$--$0.03399$, 24 matched clusters) and the prospective replication was
$0.04030$ ($0.02883$--$0.05856$, six matched clusters). Their explicitly
descriptive fixed-effect synthesis is $0.02936$ ($0.01872$--$0.03999$). This
synthesis summarizes consistency; it does not transform the discovery sample
into confirmatory evidence or add a new V14 memory experiment.

\section{Controlled quenches and recovery}

The three disruptions isolate environmental, topological, and informational
changes. Field reversal changes every private field and then restores it.
Partition removes cross-community delivery paths while preserving within-group
communication and message opportunities. Corruption flips categorical content
for a preassigned half of senders while preserving payload length and delivery
count. Agents see only the resulting local evidence; the disruption label is
not prompted.

A leave-one-cluster-out nominal manifold is fit from undisturbed trajectories
using training-cluster-only standardization and a shrinkage Mahalanobis metric
on $Z_t$. For abrupt quenches we measure response---not early warning: maximum
departure, path length, time outside the nominal region, overshoot, recovery
time, final residual, and the signed route area. Field reversal changes maximum
post-quench departure relative to nominal evolution by \VFourteenHtwo{}
(95\% interval \VFourteenHtwoCI). Its early counter-quench peak exceeds the
final-five-sweep mean by \VFourteenHthree{} (\VFourteenHthreeCI). These values
are coordinates in the frozen regularized metric, not universal physical
scales.

Every field-reversal cluster has a positive H2 paired contrast. Mean maximum
post-quench departure is $137.73$ for field reversal and $3.92$ for nominal
evolution (exact one-sided sign-flip $p=0.015625$; Holm-adjusted $p=0.046875$).
Restoration generates a second departure pulse before all six
trajectories return below their held-out-cluster-excluded training-nominal thresholds exactly six sweeps
later; the mean final-five-sweep distance is $1.56$. The frozen H3 contrast is
$134.11$ (nominal exact $p=0.015625$; Holm-adjusted $p=0.046875$), but because
peak-minus-final distance is nonnegative by construction, that sign-flip
probability is not standalone evidence. The threshold crossing, small residual,
and full time-resolved counter-quench path are consistent with finite-time
return toward the restored nominal regime.
Partition and corruption remain near nominal in this design, so the result is
specific to a private-field quench rather than an omnibus disruption detector.

\section{Quantifying information in the reduced state}

Three representations are frozen before formal execution. The order-only model
uses belief and action magnetization plus belief--action overlap. The simple
uncertainty model uses confidence, individual uncertainty, and disagreement.
The full model adds configuration entropy, entropy rate, total correlation,
mutual information, effective energy, energy fluctuations, susceptibility,
spatial correlation, and temporal coordinates. A regularized multinomial
logistic model is trained under
leave-one-graph-cluster-out cross-validation; no observable is selected on a
held-out trajectory.

The full representation's cluster-held-out balanced accuracy is \FullAccuracy,
versus \OrderAccuracy{} for order only; the simple-uncertainty representation
also reaches $0.458$. The full-versus-order paired difference is
\VFourteenHfour{} (95\% interval \VFourteenHfourCI; exact one-sided sign-flip
$p=0.03125$; Holm-adjusted $p=0.046875$). Cluster predictions,
confusion matrices, feature-family deletions, coefficient stability, and
cluster-preserving permutations determine whether any increment is robust or
driven by one family. The delayed, full-pipeline 10,000-replicate permutation
audit gives $p=0.0030$ for full minus order-only accuracy, but $p=0.5963$ for
full minus simple uncertainty; the full representation therefore separates
conditions beyond order-only summaries without outperforming the much smaller
uncertainty representation. This is not a claim that entropy outperforms a controller;
it asks whether jointly measured fluctuation, dependence, compatibility, and
temporal coordinates retain trajectory structure absent from order averages.

The result is specific rather than omnibus. The full model identifies all six
held-out field reversals, compared with one for order only and five for simple
uncertainty, but nominal, partition, and corruption remain mutually confusable.
Across 276 prespecified distance sensitivities, 266 preserve a positive
field-minus-nominal contrast and ten are exactly zero; none reverse the
ordering. Removing the entropy/dependence family nearly eliminates the mean
contrast ($0.056$ distance units), whereas removing energy, order, response,
uncertainty, or temporal families preserves a large contrast in the primary
shrinkage geometry. This identifies dependence and entropy as important to the
distance construction, while the accuracy tie prevents a claim that the full
state dominates the much smaller uncertainty representation. Prespecified
three- and seven-sweep refits and every single-observable deletion were omitted
from the original implementation and completed after formal outcomes in a
versioned audit. They do not replace the five-sweep primary estimator. The
field-minus-nominal ordering remains positive for three-, five-, and
seven-sweep geometries and after every single-coordinate deletion; deleting
pairwise mutual information yields the smallest single-deletion mean contrast,
$89.71$ metric units.

\section{Effective-model comparison}

The immutable V13 comparison found that the fitted kinetic surrogate missed the
direct LLM coupling direction and overestimated the noise contrast. V14 retains
that negative boundary. Adding persistence or semantic indicator terms is
considered only as a low-dimensional out-of-sample diagnostic, not as a
post-hoc fit target. Persistent memory, heterogeneous fields, belief--action
lag, saturation, state-dependent response, asymmetric message use, and prompt
context are therefore candidate coordinates where the simple closure fails.

\section{Limits of thermodynamic interpretation}

Several distinctions are essential. First, binary coordinates are measured
choices, but the LLM's hidden computation is not a binary spin. Second,
$H_{\rm ref}$ is an effective observable defined by the analyst; no Gibbs
stationary distribution is asserted. Third, decoding temperature alters token
sampling and is called decision noise. Fourth, total correlation and entropy
rate are information measures whose estimates depend on finite windows and
coarse-graining. Fifth, time-reversal divergence becomes exact Markov entropy
production only under closure and stationarity assumptions not generally met
by these data. Finally, $N=8,16$ direct networks cannot establish a
thermodynamic limit.

The fixed-degree modular topology has a perfect cross-community matching. This
is analytically convenient and makes partition restoration exact, but is not a
generic social graph. One open-weight model, symmetric abstract alternatives,
and short bounded outputs limit external validity. Natural-language semantics
are deliberately constrained to obtain a tractable microstate; richer tasks
would introduce additional slow and hidden coordinates.

\section{Discussion}

The scientific value of this framework lies in compression with explicit
boundaries. A local response coefficient connects delivered state to a network
coupling. Magnetization identifies order, susceptibility identifies collective
fluctuation, autocorrelation identifies persistence, and multi-information
separates dependence from local uncertainty. Effective energy combines spatial,
cross-layer, and field mismatch. Path reversal detects time asymmetry that may
be invisible to static state frequencies. In the balanced field quench,
magnetization alone barely moves while entropy rate, total correlation, energy
variance, and nominal-manifold distance form two large pulses. The first marks
reorganization and the second marks the counter-quench, followed by rapid
recovery. The overlap of partition, corruption, and nominal trajectories shows
the complementary boundary: a fixed reduced representation does not guarantee
sensitivity to a weak or dynamically ineffective perturbation.

The immutable V12 nonreciprocity boundary is also informative. Directed
delivery alone was not the dominant detectable source of time asymmetry under
matched degree and traffic. In contrast, bounded memory explicitly introduces
a historical coordinate and is a plausible mechanism for persistence and
irreversibility. This motivates future theory in which message semantics and
memory are slow variables rather than treating all asymmetry as an
antisymmetric pair coupling.

Future work should add independent models, richer finite-state decisions,
larger direct sizes, longer stationary records, and preregistered semantic
perturbations. Exact small-state kernels could clarify which projected currents
are robust to hidden variables. None of these extensions is needed to interpret
the present finite-size measurements, but they are needed before universality or
thermodynamic-limit claims.

\section{Conclusion}

State-separated, locally informed LLM-agent instances with private state and
explicit message delivery form a measurable stochastic many-agent process. The
V12 coupling and decoding-noise
directions did not replicate: V13 estimates were null or reversed, and the
fitted kinetic surrogate captured only the noise direction. Bounded memory did
replicate, increasing adjusted pathwise irreversibility by $0.04030$ nats per
attempted update. The reduced energy--entropy--order representation sharply
tracked field reversal and improved four-class accuracy over order-only
variables, but tied the simple-uncertainty representation and left partition,
corruption, and nominal operation weakly separated. Its clearest added value is
mechanistic: it resolves dependence and temporal reorganization when the
population mean remains close to zero. Statistical mechanics is therefore
useful here as a finite-size, reduced descriptive framework with demonstrated
memory and quench sensitivity, not as a universal response law or a promise
that thermodynamic quantities improve agent performance.

\section{Random-sequential transition protocol}

For every sweep, a deterministic seed generates a random permutation of all
$N$ identities. At opportunity $k$, only the scheduled agent's prompt is
constructed. Its inbox contains the latest packets actually delivered to that
identity; a peer's private field, memory, and undelivered outbox are absent.
The validated model object is decoded through the panel's latent-label map and
applied to that agent. Its selected packet is serialized, one recipient is
drawn from the current delivery row, and delivery occurs before opportunity
$k+1$. Consequently, updates within one sweep are temporally linked, while
each identity receives exactly one opportunity per sweep.

Matched arms reuse graph, initial state, private fields, label map, opportunity
permutations, recipient variates, and model seeds. The perturbation changes
only the declared factor. During a partition, cross-community paths are
removed and remaining row weights renormalized. During corruption, categorical
packet fields from the preassigned even-index sender half are flipped after
the agent constructs the packet; the agent-owned decision and outbox are not
rewritten. Restoration resets fields and delivery exactly and disables the
channel transform.

\section{Information estimators}

Let $Y_t$ denote the fixed coarse macrostate word. For observed counts
$n(y_{t-k:t})$, the order-$k$ conditional entropy estimator is
\begin{equation}
\widehat h_k=-\sum_{y_{t-k:t-1}}\widehat p(y_{t-k:t-1})
\sum_{y_t}\widehat p_{\delta}(y_t\mid y_{t-k:t-1})
\log \widehat p_{\delta}(y_t\mid y_{t-k:t-1}),
\end{equation}
where $\delta$ is the frozen pseudocount. Total correlation is evaluated on
the joint belief--action word over the same rolling window. These plug-in
quantities describe the chosen coarse-graining; neither is extrapolated to an
unobserved microscopic entropy.

The reversal estimator forms length-$L$ words, computes smoothed forward and
reverse empirical probabilities on their union support, and divides the KL
divergence by $L-1$ attempted-update intervals. Two hundred within-trajectory
time permutations establish the finite-length occupancy-matched floor. The
primary adjusted statistic subtracts that mean without truncation, so negative
values are possible. Confidence intervals resample complete matched clusters,
not overlapping words.

\section{Trajectory-level representation comparison}

The disruption classifier receives one feature vector for each complete
panel, constructed from baseline-to-disruption changes and recovery residuals.
All four panels from one graph/environment cluster are withheld together.
Scaling and multinomial-logistic coefficients are fit only on the other
clusters. Thus windows from a held-out trajectory never enter model fitting,
nominal-manifold estimation, or feature selection. The simple, order-only, and
full feature sets are frozen in the protocol; their comparison is an ablation
of collective representation, not a controller competition.

\section{Agent autonomy and information-boundary audit}

Agent autonomy is an experimental constraint rather than a label attached to
the simulator.  Each identity is represented by a separate state object and a
separate bounded inbox, outbox, memory list, private field, workload, belief,
action, confidence, and commitment.  Prompt construction accepts the scheduled
identity and a read-only view of that object's delivered information.  It has no
argument through which evaluator-only $X_t$, peer memory, or an undelivered
packet can enter.  The scheduler supplies only an update opportunity and a
recipient draw.  It does not supply a proposed scientific state.

Three counterfactual tests enforce this boundary.  First, perturbing one
agent's private field or memory may change that agent's serialized prompt but
must leave every peer-private digest invariant.  Second, a deliberately invalid
model object may trigger one syntax repair, after which failure produces a
recorded no-change transition rather than a central replacement.  Third,
partition and restoration modify only the delivery operator: agent-owned
outboxes and state remain unchanged.  Formal trajectories record peer-mutation
counts, message opportunities, delivered packets, complete wire bytes, and
content-addressed raw-decision digests.  Any privacy mutation or scheduler
substitution invalidates the run rather than becoming an exclusion.

Latent alternatives are counterbalanced independently of the scientific
factors.  Display order, the mapping from displayed words to $\pm1$, initial
beliefs and actions, and private-field signs are balanced by frozen seeds.  The
model output is decoded only after schema validation.  Consequently, a
display-side preference remains measurable as a nuisance response but cannot
systematically define high coupling, high noise, memory, or disruption arms.

\section{Quench operators and matched restoration}

Let $\mathcal P_0$ denote the baseline transition operator induced by the
fixed graph, local fields, update schedule, and clean message transform.  Each
quench replaces exactly one component during sweeps 16--30 and restores the
original object for sweeps 31--45.  Field reversal uses
$\bm h\mapsto-\bm h$ while leaving communication unchanged.  The partition
uses a masked delivery matrix
\begin{equation}
W^{\rm part}_{ij}=W_{ij}\mathbf 1[c(i)=c(j)]
\end{equation}
followed by row normalization over remaining eligible neighbors.  Opportunity
counts remain fixed even when a cross-community delivery is unavailable.
Message corruption applies a deterministic content transform to the
preassigned half of senders after packet serialization and before delivery;
the number and encoded length of packets are unchanged.  The undisturbed arm
retains $\mathcal P_0$ throughout.

Recovery is therefore a return of the exogenous operator, not a reset of the
agent configuration.  Residual displacement measures state retained by the
collective after the drive is removed.  Because these are abrupt, known
interventions, departure at or after the boundary is a response measure and
cannot be interpreted as prospective early warning.

\section{Frozen hypothesis tests and engineering audit}

The V14 engineering pilot was limited to parser validity, latent occupancy,
transition-direction counts, perturbation timing, privacy, delivery, runtime,
and token projection. It comprised 256 decisions and was prohibited from
inspecting quench effects. H2 uses one maximum-departure contrast per matched
cluster; historical H3 uses one within-field-reversal maximum-minus-final
restoration contrast per cluster; H4 uses one full-minus-order-only held-out accuracy
contrast per cluster. Exhaustive sign flips enumerate all $2^6$ paired null
assignments. Ten thousand cluster-bootstrap draws provide intervals, and Holm
correction controls the H2--H4 family.

Two retained pilot process failures occurred before protocol freeze. The first
stopped before any decision because deterministic CUDA execution lacked the
documented cuBLAS workspace variable. The second completed all 256 decisions
but encountered a configuration-key mismatch during summary construction.
Rather than exceed the 300-decision pilot ceiling, the retained responses were
content-addressed and deterministically replayed, validating every prompt,
seed, and sampling temperature. No prompt, scientific threshold, perturbation,
estimator, or formal seed changed. The frozen protocol records both the pilot
summary and the execution-source checksum.

A source audit after the freeze identified implementation omissions and a
recovery-threshold mismatch. The original code saved three- and seven-sweep
macrostate series but fitted nominal distance only for the primary five-sweep
series; it implemented observable-family rather than every single-coordinate
deletion; and its recovery threshold used the held-out field trajectory's own
baseline rather than the protocol-declared training-cluster nominal ensemble.
A versioned delayed audit preserves the committed historical outputs, then
refits every rolling window and covariance fold without held-out data, performs
all single-coordinate deletions, and applies the training-only threshold to
recovery. No frozen trajectory, primary H2/H4 estimator, seed, or historical
Holm value changes. These sensitivities are explicitly post-outcome completions
of prespecified analyses, not new prospective evidence.

\section{Nominal manifold and trajectory geometry}

For held-out quench cluster $g$, the nominal center $\widehat\mu_{-g}$ and
ridge-regularized covariance $\widehat\Sigma_{-g}$ are estimated only from
undisturbed trajectories in the other clusters.  The standardized departure is
\begin{equation}
d_{g,t}=\left[(Z_{g,t}-\widehat\mu_{-g})^T
\widehat\Sigma_{-g}^{-1}(Z_{g,t}-\widehat\mu_{-g})\right]^{1/2}.
\end{equation}
Its nominal 95th percentile is likewise learned without cluster $g$.
Maximum departure, time outside that region, final residual, and the first two
consecutive recovery points below threshold are computed at trajectory level.
The energy--entropy loop area uses the polygonal path in temporal order and is
a finite dynamical hysteresis descriptor; it is not thermodynamic work.

The representation comparison uses prospectively fixed trajectory summaries.
All four conditions sharing one graph and environment are withheld together.
A fixed $L_2$-regularized multinomial logistic model and training-fold
standardization prevent the full representation from receiving a different
model family. The analysis reports balanced accuracy, macro recall, confusion
matrices, graph-level predictions, feature-group deletions, and the paired
full-minus-order-only contrast. With six held-out clusters, coefficient
patterns remain finite-sample and are not promoted to universal biomarkers.

\section{Estimator and coarse-graining sensitivity}

The primary block length is three and pseudocount is 0.5. Sensitivities use
block lengths two and four and pseudocounts 0.1 and 1.0. Configuration bins are
fixed before formal execution. Time-shuffled paths preserve length and marginal
occupancy while destroying temporal order; their mean reversal divergence is
subtracted as the primary bias floor. These corrections can yield a slightly
negative adjusted value, which is retained rather than truncated.

The dependence audit independently circular-shifts each belief/action variable
within a trajectory. This preserves each variable's marginal occupancy and
within-variable temporal structure while breaking synchronous cross-variable
dependence. Raw total correlation, its mean null floor, their untruncated
difference, and a normalized coordinate are all retained for three-, five-, and
seven-sweep windows. Pairwise and graph-edge mutual information receive the
same treatment. Synthetic independent processes return adjusted estimates near
zero, while coupled processes remain positive.
