# Paper summary

V14 studies collective memory and controlled quench response in networks of actual independent Qwen agents. The microscopic process preserves separate contexts, private state, typed authority, explicit message delivery, and random-sequential local updates. Statistical-mechanical observables form a finite-size reduced state rather than a claim of literal thermodynamics.

The immutable V12 discovery memory effect was 0.01790 (95% CI 0.00357 to 0.03399) and the V13 prospective replication was 0.04030 (95% CI 0.02883 to 0.05856) nats/update. Their descriptive fixed-effect synthesis is 0.02936 (95% CI 0.01872 to 0.03999); no new V14 memory trajectory enters it. V14 adds six new matched quench clusters. Field reversal changed maximum post-quench macrostate departure relative to nominal by 133.805 (95% CI 107.949 to 184.075). All six panels returned below their held-out nominal threshold six sweeps after restoration. The frozen peak-minus-final H3 contrast was 134.110 (95% CI 106.654 to 184.196), but its sign is structurally nonnegative and is not standalone evidence. The full representation's LOCO balanced-accuracy increment over order-only features was 0.333 (95% CI 0.167 to 0.458).

The contribution is the integrated microstate-to-macrostate formulation, bias-aware temporal-asymmetry analysis, field quench/counter-quench paths, nominal-manifold robustness audit, and transparent representation ablation. Under the balanced field quench, population magnetization remains near zero while entropy rate, total correlation, and effective-energy variance rise strongly, exposing reorganization missed by a mean-state count. The full and simple-uncertainty representations both reached 0.458 balanced accuracy, so V14 supports full-over-order information but not full-over-uncertainty superiority. Partition and corruption remained weakly separated from nominal. Results are finite-size and model-specific; reference energy and reversal divergence remain effective coarse-grained observables.
