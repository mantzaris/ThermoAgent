# Prospective V8 stop decision

The final corrected trigger pilot failed the predeclared requirement that mean
pre-disruption, non-initial transmission rate be at most 0.10 in each primary
application. Observed rates ranged from 0.712 to 0.856 across the two declared
thresholds and applications. Neither candidate was eligible.

Consequently, V8 did not freeze a confirmatory protocol, train the planned five
RL seeds, run formal development, open validation, or open the locked holdout.
This decision preserves the negative mechanism evidence and cannot be reversed
by post hoc threshold tuning within V8.
