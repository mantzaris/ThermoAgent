## Claims-to-evidence rule

No sentence may use “validated,” “generalizes,” “human operators,” or
“confirmatory” for v4 outcomes. The strongest permissible wording is
“development evidence suggests,” with exact table/figure references. Gate 3,
the commercial null, and the absence of later stages must appear in the
abstract, results, discussion, and conclusion.
