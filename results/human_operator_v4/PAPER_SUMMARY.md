# Paper-oriented summary: ThermoHITL v4

## Working title

**Distributed Thermodynamic Observability for Allocating Scarce Human Attention
in Decentralized Autonomous Systems**

## Evidence classification

Development-stage boundary study with simulated operators and a six-episode
real-Qwen actionability qualification. Validation, learned-policy training, and
confirmatory holdout were prospectively not run because Gate 3 failed. This
summary is not a claim-ready AIJ abstract.

## Honest provisional abstract

Independent autonomous systems can expose a supervisor to fragmented and
inconsistent local beliefs that ordinary severity indicators do not summarize.
We implement ThermoHITL v4, a privacy-preserving oversight architecture in
which commercial, humanitarian, and abstract utility-restoration agents gossip
bounded entropy sketches, request scarce simulated-operator attention, and
respond to typed interventions. A pre-specified development study uses matched
KPI severity, counterfactual branching, group-separated feature-block models,
and cluster-aware inference. KPI-plus-entropy/disagreement improved budgeted
causal intervention utility over KPI-only by 0.159 (95% cluster-bootstrap CI
0.062–0.261) in humanitarian logistics and 0.179 (0.103–0.261) in utility
restoration, while the commercial boundary application showed exactly zero
gain. The incremental benefit vanished under globally public information and
was not reproduced by within-severity permutations. Bounded interventions
completed 32 and 34 beneficial causal chains in the two positive applications.
However, the prerequisite coordination-necessity gate failed: fixed
communication reduced utility-restoration loss by 4.43%, below the frozen 5%
practical threshold. The study therefore stopped before validation, RL
training, or holdout. The results motivate distributed observability as a
conditional hypothesis but do not provide confirmatory evidence or evidence
about real human operators.

## Verified contributions at this stage

1. A third, defensive abstract utility-restoration simulator with distinct
   service, telemetry/communication, and restoration-logistics layers.
2. Independent agents with role-scoped authority, private state, explicit
   negotiation, bounded human escalation, conservation, and exact replay.
3. A deployable-view schema that exposes only authorized distributed summaries
   and hashes the payload consumed by the simulated operator.
4. A matched-KPI causal-ranking design separating visible severity from belief
   fragmentation and disagreement.
5. Development evidence that thermodynamic observability can add selection
   value under fragmented information in two applications, plus an exact-null
   commercial boundary and a prospective no-go decision.

Only items 1–4 are stable engineering/method contributions. Item 5 requires a
new validation/holdout study before it can support a substantive journal claim.

## Primary numerical results

- Formal deterministic development: 1,584/1,584 episodes complete.
- Real Qwen qualification: 6/6 first-pass valid and demand/service-reaching.
- Replay: 1,590/1,590 exact; zero mismatches; maximum conservation residual 0.
- Coordination reduction from fixed communication: 24.92% commercial, 8.59%
  humanitarian, 4.43% utility restoration (frozen target: 5% in every app).
- Simulated-operator loss reduction: 0.91% commercial, 20.96% humanitarian,
  13.42% utility restoration.
- Thermodynamic causal-utility gain over KPI-only: 0 commercial; 0.1589
  humanitarian; 0.1787 utility restoration.
- Harmful interventions in the formal causal matrix: 0.
- Complete causal chains: 2, 32, and 34 respectively.
- Validation/RL/holdout: not run.

## Limitations

- Development data informed simulator correction; no fresh validation exists.
- Gate 3 missed by 0.57 percentage points in utility restoration.
- The operator is simulated, deterministic, and high-accuracy.
- No low-confidence decision exercised safe abstention.
- Only six Qwen episodes qualify real-model actionability; comparative matrices
  use deterministic independent planners.
- No learned v4 method or multi-seed stability result exists.
- Abstract cyber-physical events do not validate real utility operation or
  cybersecurity.

## Journal readiness

Not ready for an AIJ full research article. The methods and boundary result can
support a preregistered successor study, but the intended empirical
contribution requires a new version that passes its own prospective gates and
then completes validation, multiple training seeds, and a fresh holdout.
