# Proposed future human study — not conducted

No participant was recruited, observed, or analyzed in v4. This document is
technical preparation only. The responsible institution must obtain an
appropriate ethics/IRB determination before recruitment or data collection.
Simulated-operator effects are not human effect-size estimates.

## Question and conditions

Under a fixed attention budget, does a KPI-plus-distributed-thermodynamic view
improve human incident prioritization relative to the same KPI-only view?
Use a preregistered, blocked, within-participant crossover with matched scenario
panels and two primary conditions:

1. local KPI only; and
2. the identical KPI payload plus distributed entropy, belief disagreement,
   and consensus confidence.

Energy-only, thermodynamic-only, oracles, and alternative visual encodings may
appear only in a separately powered secondary family. Oracle/evaluator-global
data must never be shown as a deployable condition.

## Applications and scenario matching

Use humanitarian and abstract utility-restoration scenarios as primary domains;
retain commercial logistics as a boundary domain. Match exogenous randomness,
incident timing, operator slots, intervention menu, action cost, communication
state, and visible KPI severity. Separate practice, pilot, and confirmatory
seeds. Counterbalance view order and scenario assignment with the adjacent
randomization template.

## Candidate endpoints

One primary endpoint must be chosen before data collection, preferably service
loss avoided per active operator minute. Secondary outcomes may include:

- correct incident prioritization;
- decision and response time;
- harmful intervention rate;
- missed critical incidents;
- calibration/Brier score;
- NASA-TLX or another validated workload instrument;
- trust, over-reliance, and uncertainty comprehension; and
- final service outcome under a fixed attention budget.

Participant and matched environment panel must both be modeled as independent
clusters/random effects as appropriate. Clicks and intervention probes are not
independent experimental units.

## Power-analysis template

The adjacent `future_human_power_analysis_template.csv` must be completed from
a blinded human pilot, not v4 simulated-operator effects. Freeze the minimally
important effect, alpha/multiplicity family, target power, within-participant
correlation, attrition, exclusion rules, technical-retry policy, and sample
size before confirmatory recruitment.

## Procedure and safeguards

- Obtain informed consent and permit withdrawal without penalty.
- Explain that all events and agents are abstract simulations.
- Never expose private agent vaults or evaluator/counterfactual state.
- Use pseudonymous participant IDs; keep consent linkage separately.
- Log the exact hashed view, available action menu, action, timestamps, and
  simulator outcome using `future_human_trial_schema.json`.
- Predefine accessibility accommodations, fatigue limits, breaks,
  compensation, data retention, and adverse-event handling.
- Debrief participants on simulated agents and the nonphysical meaning of
  entropy and energy.
- Do not infer real utility cybersecurity or humanitarian operational safety.

## Prepared instrumentation

The replay dashboard supports play/pause/step/rewind, jump-to-alert, condition
and scenario selection through episode artifacts, bounded action display,
payload hashing, response-time integration points, matched branch provenance,
and SVG/PDF export. The schema includes questionnaire fields but contains no
fabricated responses. A later interactive action-submission endpoint must
undergo a separate security/privacy review before participant use.
