# 20–30 page AIJ-style paper outline (not a manuscript)

This outline is ready to populate from repository artifacts but should not be
treated as a submission plan until stronger validation and holdout evidence
exist.

1. **Introduction (2–3 pages)** — scarce oversight, fragmented autonomous
   systems, narrow conditional hypothesis, V4 motivation, V5 prospective stop.
2. **Related work (3–4 pages)** — adjustable autonomy, human-on-the-loop
   supervision, event-triggered control, multi-agent communication, uncertainty
   and disagreement, causal triage, logistics and restoration abstractions.
3. **Problem formulation (2 pages)** — independent agents, private information,
   operator budget, matched causal utility, communication cost, applications.
4. **Thermodynamic observability (2–3 pages)** — belief entropy, entropy
   dispersion, JS disagreement, distributed estimator, confidence, secondary
   energy/free energy, privacy boundary and communication accounting.
5. **Agents and environments (3–4 pages)** — commercial boundary,
   humanitarian logistics, cyber-physical utility restoration, typed tools,
   intervention cost/harm, stochastic tape, conservation and replay.
6. **Oversight policies and dashboard (2 pages)** — KPI, feature blocks,
   budgeted ranking, abstention, simulated operator, oracle boundaries,
   functional replay exports.
7. **Prospective protocol and statistics (2–3 pages)** — development iterations,
   grouped cross-fitting, independent clusters, 10,000 bootstraps, refit
   permutation, gates, multiple seeds, validation/holdout lock.
8. **Engineering and agent qualification (1–2 pages)** — tests, replay,
   deterministic actionability, Qwen qualification, privacy, limitations.
9. **Development results (4–5 pages)** — coordination necessity, human causal
   usefulness, failed primary incremental value, feature blocks, fragmentation
   interaction, trigger failure, communication cost, abstention safety.
10. **Learned-policy results (1–2 pages)** — five seeds per method, learning
    curves, single-action collapse, why this fails rather than supports Gate 10.
11. **Mechanistic and falsification analyses (2 pages)** — overlap, absence of
    univariate shortcut, public-information control, refit permutations,
    regime heterogeneity, candidate harm.
12. **Discussion and boundary conditions (2–3 pages)** — what entropy measures,
    when it fails to add actionability, KPI sufficiency, confidence abstention,
    implications for benchmark design.
13. **Limitations, ethics, and cyber scope (1–2 pages)** — simulated operators,
    no human subjects, no real infrastructure, abstract humanitarian model,
    post-development secondary correction.
14. **Conclusion (0.5–1 page)** — honest no-go and prerequisites for another
    prospective study.
