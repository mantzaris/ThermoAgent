# Paper-oriented V5 summary

## Working title

**Distributed Entropic Observability for Budgeted Human Oversight of
Decentralized Autonomous Agents Under Fragmented Information: A Prospective
Development Boundary Study**

## Provisional abstract

Independent autonomous agents can hold private and conflicting evidence while
a human supervisor has capacity to review only a few incidents. We implemented
a three-domain simulator and prospectively tested whether distributed local
belief entropy and inter-agent Jensen–Shannon disagreement improve budgeted
causal intervention selection beyond same-boundary operational KPIs. The
development design used four concurrent incidents per panel, costly imperfect
interventions, matched stochastic counterfactuals, explicit sketch traffic,
20 grouped environment seeds, separate commercial, humanitarian, and abstract
utility-restoration applications, five decentralized RL seeds per method, and
108 real-Qwen decisions. Coordination and bounded simulated-operator
intervention were consequential, and low-confidence abstention reduced harmful
choices. However, KPI-plus-entropy/disagreement produced lower mean causal
utility than KPI-only in humanitarian (−0.0116, 95% CI −0.0279 to 0.0043) and
utility restoration (−0.0100, −0.0277 to 0.0068), while the prespecified
fragmentation interaction was uncertain. Trigger false activations were high,
real-Qwen actionability missed frozen thresholds, and both learned methods
collapsed to a single no-action policy. Required development gates therefore
failed; validation and the sealed holdout were not run. These results delimit
the conditions under which distributed uncertainty summaries are descriptive
or safety-relevant but not incrementally actionable for scarce oversight.

## Verified contributions

- A reproducible multi-incident, matched-counterfactual oversight benchmark
  spanning commercial, humanitarian, and defensive abstract utility systems.
- Explicit independent-agent privacy, authority, negotiation, commitments,
  typed actions, partitions, sketch accounting, conservation, and replay.
- A cluster-aware, budgeted causal-utility evaluation that removes V4's
  single-verification shortcut and refits the full pipeline in permutation
  falsification.
- A negative primary result and a positive narrow safety result: distributed
  entropy/disagreement did not beat KPIs, while low-consensus abstention reduced
  harmful choices.
- A prospective gate mechanism that prevented weak development evidence from
  reaching validation or holdout.

## Prohibited claims

V5 does not establish thermodynamic superiority, real-human benefit, reliable
learned coordination, confirmatory generalization, operational utility or
humanitarian safety, or cyber-defense efficacy. It does not justify an AIJ
submission around the intended positive claim.

## Figure and table plan

Use architecture, three-application overview, utility network, phase plane,
feature-block utility, primary forest, fragmentation interaction, sketch-cost
Pareto, abstention, RL seeds, calibration, and causal-population figures in the
main paper. Place dashboard exports, trigger burden, regime heterogeneity,
counterfactual effect distributions, Qwen actionability, full gate table,
permutation diagnostics, and replay/QA details in supplemental material.

## Recommended disposition

The package is adequate for an engineering demonstration and could support a
workshop or conference boundary paper emphasizing falsification and benchmark
design. It is not ready for a full journal manuscript or Artificial
Intelligence submission without a new prospectively validated mechanism.
