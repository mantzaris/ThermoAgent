# Proposed future human study — not conducted

No participant was recruited, observed, or analyzed in V5. This file is
technical preparation only. Obtain the responsible institution's ethics/IRB
determination before recruitment or collection.

Use a preregistered, blocked, counterbalanced comparison between (A) the
authorized KPI-only dashboard and (B) the identical payload plus distributed
belief entropy, Jensen–Shannon disagreement, and consensus confidence. Keep
scenario, stochastic tape, intervention menu, attention slots, latency, and
budget matched. Humanitarian and abstract utility-restoration panels are the
primary domains; commercial is a prespecified boundary.

Choose one primary endpoint before data collection, preferably service loss
avoided per active operator minute. Secondary endpoints may include correct
incident prioritization, decision time, harmful intervention rate, missed
critical incidents, calibration, NASA-TLX, trust and over-reliance, uncertainty
comprehension, and service outcome under a fixed budget.

Estimate sample size from a blinded human pilot—not from V5's simulated
operators. Freeze the minimally important effect, multiplicity family,
within-person correlation, power, attrition, exclusions, and technical retry
rules before confirmatory recruitment. Treat participant and matched scenario
panel as clusters; clicks and candidate actions are not independent units.

Log pseudonymous participant ID, condition, randomized sequence, hashed view
payload, available actions, selected action, response time, operator minutes,
questionnaire responses, and simulator outcome. Keep consent linkage separate,
permit withdrawal, define breaks and fatigue limits, and never expose private
agent vaults, evaluator state, future disruptions, stochastic tape, or
counterfactual outcomes to the participant.
