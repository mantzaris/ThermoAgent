# Paper-facing V11 summary

## Working title

**Evidence-grounded belief revision as a prerequisite for entropy-production
measurements in decentralized LLM-agent networks**

## Contribution

V11 replaces an unidentifiable binary message-use test with a controlled latent
state, likelihood-bearing typed evidence, continuous belief elicitation,
matched counterfactual prompts, and panel-level inference. It demonstrates a
measurable average evidence-induced log-odds shift in Qwen across route and
repair framings, while showing why that fact is insufficient for a
statistical-mechanical network claim.

The decisive qualification was frozen before its 864 requests. Although its
pooled directional and placebo-adjusted endpoints passed, reliability
monotonicity and binary transition diversity failed. The formal
reciprocal/nonreciprocal network experiment was therefore never opened. The
result is a useful methodological boundary: continuous self-reports can reveal
message effects hidden by binary switching, but those reports may remain
uncalibrated, directionally asymmetric, and unsuitable for empirical Markov
kernels.

## Primary numbers

- 48 independent matched information clusters; 864 requests; 886 model calls.
- Signed log-odds effect: 0.1343 (95% CI 0.0915--0.1811).
- Placebo-adjusted effect: 0.1343 (95% CI 0.0905--0.1816).
- Route viability: 0.1886 (95% CI 0.1164--0.2684).
- Repair hypothesis: 0.0799 (95% CI 0.0445--0.1197).
- Reliability means: 0.0463, 0.1686, 0.1779, 0.1388.
- Normative-LLR slope: 0.04965; frozen minimum 0.05.
- Right-choice fraction: 0.9224; frozen maximum 0.90.
- First-pass/after-repair validity: 97.45%/99.88%.
- Calibration: ECE 0.2955; Brier 0.0968.

## Scientific disposition

The evidence supports a qualification-stage claim about average continuous
belief revision, not LLM entropy production. The correct paper is presently a
methodology and boundary-result article anchored to the exact V10 theory. A
positive JSTAT claim about entropy production in actual LLM-agent networks
requires a new, prospectively qualified elicitation design that removes the
direction bias and yields diverse empirical transitions.
