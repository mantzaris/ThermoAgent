# Paper summary

V12 studies random-sequential stochastic dynamics in networks of genuinely independent Qwen agents. Each model call chooses a local categorical belief, action, commitment, bounded memory update, typed tool action, and outgoing signal from only its private field, explicit local state, and delivered inbox. A divergence-free antisymmetric circulation varies communication reciprocity while preserving support, weighted in/out degree, payload size, and update opportunity count.

The experiment connects three levels: the exact V10 heat-bath reference, a fitted decentralized kinetic-Ising surrogate, and direct Qwen trajectories. The prospective formal study contains 44,352 decisions across 400 network trajectories plus a 576-decision microscopic response grid. Exact deterministic regeneration reproduced every recorded transition.

The strongest result is microscopic. Delivered neighbor field changed latent-plus choice by 0.08333 per unit field (95% CI 0.04167 to 0.125; 96 independent information-state clusters), after option order, semantic label, and latent mapping were counterbalanced. Increasing coupling raised finite-size order, susceptibility, and correlation time; increasing decoding noise reduced all three after Holm correction.

The central network-level nonreciprocity result is negative. At `alpha=0.8`, bias-adjusted length-three path irreversibility differed from reciprocal communication by -0.0002327 nats/update in small systems (95% CI -0.001441 to 0.001013) and -0.0007589 in collective systems (-0.01115 to 0.009718). Dose response, quadratic predictive advantage, and orientation/size replication were not supported. A positive projected Markov-current contrast in the small system is retained only as a secondary diagnostic because state support was sparse and first-order closure was not established generally.

The defensible contribution is therefore a full empirical statistical-mechanics characterization of decentralized LLM microdynamics, including a causal local influence law, finite-size collective order/fluctuation response, effective-model comparison, memory effects, probability currents, entropy, and carefully bias-corrected pathwise irreversibility. Exact entropy-production statements remain restricted to the heat-bath reference; direct LLM quantities are explicitly coarse-grained and finite-size.

Publication status: a serious JSTAT-oriented negative/mixed-result manuscript package, suitable for technical circulation and submission after external statistical-physics review and, ideally, longer and more diverse trajectory/model replication. It does not support a positive claim that nonreciprocity increases LLM-agent entropy production.
